About Us

The Supreme Lord Maha Vishnu is the source of all divine incarnations within the universe, incarnating to re-establish righteousness and protect the souls of his devotees. Lord Swaminarayan incarnated on April 2nd, 1781, in Chhapaiya village, Uttar Pradesh, India, to establish Bhagavat Dharma. This natural way of life, based on Vedic wisdom, is also known as Vedic Sanatan Dharma and consists of Dharma (moral duty), Gyan (knowledge), Vairagya (detachment from Maya), and Bhakti (devotional love of God).

Guruparampara: Our Gunatit Tradition

Lord Swaminarayan taught the Vedic Dharma to uplift humanity, sharing this wisdom through his books: the Shikshapatri (letter of direction), Vachanamrutham (words of nectar), and Satsangi Jivan (an authorized biography by Shatanand Muni). He was supported by five hundred sadhus (saints)—all liberated souls and Vedic scholars.

Propagating True Wisdom

In 1948, His Holiness Shastriji Maharaj Dharmajivandasji Swami established Swaminarayan Gurukul, Rajkot Sansthan, in Gujarat, India. As a pioneer within the Udhav Sampradaya, he utilized the Gurukul system to preach Vedic Dharma in its original form.

Swaminarayan Gurukul Santo Domingo

On January 27th, 1983, Shri Chaturbai Jerambhai Babaria established the International Swaminarayan Spiritual Center in Santo Domingo, DR, now known as Swaminarayan Gurukul Santo Domingo. His Holiness Shastriji Maharaj visited on August 13th, 1986, radiating divine light and transforming lives. Continuity has been maintained by Pujaya Jogi Swami Hariprakash Dasji, P Mahant Swami Devakrishna Dasji, P Devaprasad Dasji, and Devotee Dhirubhai J Babaria. Mireya Bobadilla (Ma Saraswati) notably translated the Shikshapatri and Vachanamrutam into Spanish.

Our Mandir & Mission

The mandir is open to people of all ages and walks of life. Join us for our Satsang congregation (holy fellowship) every Saturday from 5:00 PM to 7:00 PM, followed by prasad (vegetarian dinner). (Open for Satsang on Saturdays from 5:00 PM to 8:00 PM.)

Our Mission

  • Instilling self-discipline, etiquette, humanity, cultural sensibility, and love of nature and the universe.
  • Developing harmony and peace with all religions through Vedic Dharma.
  • Developing the highest character through the teaching of Vedic Dharma.
  • Providing Vedic education to anyone interested.
  • Promoting a happy and healthy life through Yoga, music, and Ayurveda (the teaching of Bhagwan Dhanvantari).
  • Teaching Bhakti Yoga for self-realization.
